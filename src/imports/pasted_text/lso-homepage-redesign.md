# LSO Group Website — Homepage Redesign & UX Refinement

Redesign and refine the existing LSO Group website homepage based on the current website content and structure.

Website:
https://lsoprojects.co.za/

## Primary objective

Transform the current LSO Group website from a basic company-profile website into a modern, premium, credible and conversion-focused digital presence for a South African construction and built-environment company.

Do NOT simply create a generic construction company template.

First understand the existing LSO website, its current messaging, services and available content. Preserve accurate and useful information, but improve the hierarchy, presentation, clarity, visual storytelling and conversion flow.

The redesigned homepage should make a visitor understand within the first 10–15 seconds:

1. Who LSO Group is
2. What LSO Group does
3. Which types of projects/clients it serves
4. Why LSO can be trusted
5. What the visitor should do next

---

# Brand positioning

Position LSO Group as a professional South African built-environment and construction partner capable of delivering projects from planning and construction through to completion and ongoing maintenance.

The visual and messaging direction should communicate:

* Professional
* Established
* Reliable
* Engineering/construction expertise
* Project delivery
* Safety
* Quality
* Accountability
* Capability
* Modern African business
* Corporate credibility

Avoid making the company look like a small residential building contractor.

The target audience should primarily feel like:

* Corporate procurement teams
* Property developers
* Government departments
* Municipalities
* SOEs
* Industrial companies
* Commercial property owners
* Facilities managers
* Project managers
* Private-sector businesses

# Visual design direction

The website should look like a serious South African construction and infrastructure company.

Avoid:

* Generic construction templates
* Excessive orange/yellow construction colours
* Stock-photo-heavy layouts
* Huge amounts of text
* Overuse of icons
* Excessive gradients
* Cheap-looking animations
* "Lorem ipsum"
* Generic marketing buzzwords
* Overly flashy effects

Prefer:

* Strong editorial typography
* Large construction photography
* Architectural imagery
* Generous whitespace
* Structured grids
* Strong visual hierarchy
* Dark/neutral professional palette
* LSO brand colours where available
* Subtle accent colour
* High-quality cards
* Subtle motion
* Premium corporate aesthetic

The design should feel closer to a modern engineering/infrastructure consultancy combined with a premium construction company than a generic builder website.

---

# UX requirements

The homepage must be easy to scan.

A visitor should be able to understand:

**Who LSO is → What LSO does → Where LSO operates → Who LSO serves → What LSO has delivered → Why LSO → How to contact LSO**

without reading every paragraph.

Use:

* Short paragraphs
* Clear headings
* Strong CTAs
* Visual hierarchy
* Cards
* Project photography
* Progressive disclosure

Do not overload the homepage.

---

# SEO requirements

Design the information architecture with SEO in mind.

The homepage should support the broader keyword strategy around:

* Construction company South Africa
* Construction company Johannesburg
* Building contractors Johannesburg
* Civil construction South Africa
* Civil contractors Gauteng
* Turnkey construction
* Project management construction
* Industrial construction
* Factory maintenance
* Factory refurbishment
* Commercial construction

Instead, create a structure where dedicated pages can target specific search intent.

/services/general-building

/services/civil-construction

/services/turnkey-projects

/services/project-management

/services/factory-maintenance

/services/drywall-ceilings

/industries/commercial

/industries/industrial

/industries/public-sector

/projects

Each page should eventually have unique, useful content.

Every major section should support the visitor's journey toward contacting LSO.

Primary conversion:

**Discuss Your Project**

Secondary conversions:

**View Projects**

**Download Company Profile**

**Request an RFQ**

**Contact LSO**

# Responsive design

Design desktop first but ensure the structure translates beautifully to:

* Desktop
* Laptop
* Tablet
* Mobile

Mobile navigation should be simple.

Cards should stack naturally.

Project imagery should remain visually strong.

Typography must remain readable.

Buttons should be touch-friendly.

# Figma Make implementation requirements

Create the homepage as a polished, realistic website prototype rather than a collection of wireframes.

Use realistic LSO content based on the existing website.

Where information is unavailable:

* Use clearly marked placeholders
* Do not invent facts
* Do not invent clients
* Do not invent project values
* Do not invent certifications
* Do not invent testimonials

Create reusable components for:

* Header
* Hero
* Trust metrics
* Service cards
* Industry cards
* Project cards
* Capability cards
* Process steps
* Article cards
* CTA sections
* Footer

Maintain consistent spacing, typography, buttons and card styles.

---

# Final design objective

The finished homepage should answer this question:

> "If I am a procurement manager, property developer, industrial company, government organisation or corporate client looking for a construction/project delivery partner, does this website give me enough confidence to contact LSO?"

If the answer is not clearly yes, refine the hierarchy, content, trust signals and conversion flow.

Prioritise **clarity, credibility, evidence and conversion over visual decoration.**
